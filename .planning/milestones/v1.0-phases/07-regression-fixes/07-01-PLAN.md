<objective>
Fix LIVE-06 (H-R3 regression gap) where live audio is inaudible on the TNP camera ("Спальня").

Purpose:
On MIUI devices (such as Xiaomi Mi 9), mono AudioTrack instances configured with AudioAttributes.CONTENT_TYPE_SPEECH suffer from an OS audio-routing bug: audio is directed exclusively to the telecommunication earpiece receiver instead of the loudspeaker, rendering camera sound virtually silent in media mode. Configuring AudioTrack with AudioAttributes.USAGE_MEDIA and AudioAttributes.CONTENT_TYPE_MUSIC ensures routing to the device media loudspeaker. Additionally, non-blocking AudioTrack write tracking and TNP audio decryption verification are added to guarantee full auditability.

Output:
Updated `AacDecoder.kt` and `AudioPipeline.kt` with robust AudioTrack configuration, byte tracking, and comprehensive unit tests.
</objective>
