<objective>
Fix EVT-04 (H-R10 regression gap: SD archive playback slideshow stutter of 3-5s per frame when jumping from events) and LIVE-07 (ensure SD quality default on Relay connections).

Purpose:
When jumping from the events screen to the SD archive via `openAt()`, or when packet drops occur over the network, `FrameAssembler` previously waited for the exact sequential missing P-frame (`lastSeq + 1`) and buffered up to `MAX_PENDING = 100` frames. Because camera GOP intervals are 3-5 seconds (60-100 frames), a single dropped P-frame froze frame delivery for the entire GOP duration until the next I-frame arrived, manifesting as a severe 3-5s slideshow.
Implementing resilient gap recovery in `FrameAssembler` (with a small reorder tolerance of 3 frames, after which the missing frame is skipped and pending frames are immediately drained) resolves the stall.
Furthermore, ensuring `resetPipeline()` is cleanly triggered on `openAt` / seek transitions clears stale live-stream frames from the pipeline, and enforcing SD quality on Relay connections fulfills requirement LIVE-07.

Output:
Updated `FrameAssembler.kt`, `PlaybackController.kt`, `QualityController.kt`, and `LiveSessionSupervisor.kt` with unit tests.
</objective>

   - Introduce `GAP_TOLERANCE = 3` (reordering window for UDP packets).
   - In `offer(unit)`:
     When a P-frame arrives with `distance > 1`:
     Enqueue the unit into `pendingBySeq`.
     If `pendingBySeq.size >= GAP_TOLERANCE`:
     The missing sequence frame has been lost on the network. Do not wait for MAX_PENDING (100 frames) or the next I-frame.
     Instead, find the earliest pending frame in `pendingBySeq` (by smallest u16 distance from `lastSeq`).
     Calculate the gap distance from `lastSeq` to that earliest frame, account for skipped frames in `dropped += gap`, advance `lastSeq` to that frame's sequence minus 1, and immediately call `drainFrom(earliestUnit)` to return all consecutive available frames to the decoder.
