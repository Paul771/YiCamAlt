<objective>
Close device defects 1 and 2 in the archive screen state.

Defect 1: after «Живой» the ruler cursor and its HH:mm:ss label stay at the last archive time and never follow the clock again. Root cause: `ArchiveUi.cursorMs` is null in Live, and TimelineRuler only moves its centre when cursorMs is non-null, so it freezes at the last archive position (before the first seek it showed the open-time centre, which looked right).
Defect 2: «Загрузка записей…» spins forever next to AuthFailed / «Повтор (n/5)» / Waiting. Root cause: the internal panel stays LOADING whenever no load has finished, regardless of whether anything is being fetched.

Output: a separate `rulerMs` field (archive position in the archive, floored wall time in Live for today), a new `ArchivePanel.IDLE` display state with a `fetching` flag, composable wiring, and virtual-time JVM tests.
</objective>
