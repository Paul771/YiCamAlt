  <how-to-verify>
    Phone unlocked, USB connected, official Yi app closed. Use the camera with an SD card (see 04-PROBE.md). If no camera has a card: insert a microSD into one camera, let it record at least an hour, or report "no card" and do only step 6.
    1. H-SD1: tap the camera -> live video; under it «Сегодня ▾». Tap it: days with recordings have a dot and can be tapped; other days are grey and cannot be tapped; pick another recorded day, then come back to today.
    2. H-SD2: the timeline shows coloured segments where the camera recorded; drag left/right scrolls, pinch zooms in/out, the red cursor stays in the centre.
    3. H-SD3: drag the timeline to a recorded moment and release -> playback from that moment within a few seconds; compare the time in the camera's own picture overlay with the «Запись HH:mm:ss» label (should match within a few seconds; a 3-hour difference = fail).
    4. H-SD4: start ~20 s before the end of a segment that has another segment after it -> playback continues into the next one without pressing anything; start ~20 s before the very last recording -> after it ends «Записи закончились» appears.
    5. H-SD5: «Пауза» freezes the picture; wait 30 s (no «Повтор» badge, no reconnect); «Продолжить» resumes from the same moment; «Живой» returns to live video (badge stays, no «Подключение…»).
    6. H-SD6: on a camera without a card -> «Нет SD-карты» where the timeline would be, live video keeps playing; pick a day without recordings (today, if nothing recorded) -> «За этот день записей нет»; if «Не удалось получить список» ever appears, press «Повторить».
    7. H-SD7: enter the archive with sound on -> sound turns off; while playing a recording press Home, come back -> archive shows «Продолжить» at the same time and does not play by itself.
    8. H-SD8: during archive playback: «На весь экран» (pause and «Живой» in the overlay), pinch zoom, snapshot -> toast «Снимок сохранён в галерею».
    9. H-SD9: open the TUTK camera: archive works like steps 1-5, or shows «Нет SD-карты» / «Архив SD на этой камере недоступен». Then say "logs ready".
